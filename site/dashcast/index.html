DashCast

Mirror your Android phone to any web browser on the same Wi-Fi, with sound and touch control. Nothing to install on the screen you're casting to.

What it does

Any browser

A laptop, a tablet, a TV or a car's built-in browser. Open an address and the phone appears.

Touch control

Tap, long-press and swipe on the browser's screen to control the phone. Back, Home and Recents buttons included.

Sound too

Music, video and game audio play through the browser. One tap turns it on.

Smooth

Up to 60 frames per second. Choose frame rate, resolution and quality to suit your Wi-Fi.

Private

Everything stays on your local network. No account, no cloud, no tracking.

Car mode

Some car browsers refuse local addresses. Car mode gives the phone a fixed address, http://6.6.6.6:8080, that they accept. Bookmark it once.

Stays put

Lock the phone to landscape or portrait while casting, and dim its screen to save battery.

How to use it

  1. Install DashCast — download the APK on your phone and open it. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
  2. Turn on touch control — in DashCast, tap Touch control and switch on "DashCast touch control" in Accessibility settings. Optional, but needed for taps, the orientation lock and dimming.
  3. Start casting — tap Start casting and choose "Entire screen" when Android asks.
  4. Open the address — on the other device, join the same Wi-Fi (or the phone's hotspot) and open the http://…:8080 address DashCast shows. Tap the speaker button for sound.

In a car

Connect the car to the phone's hotspot. In DashCast, switch Car mode on before you start casting and allow the VPN when Android asks. Then open http://6.6.6.6:8080 in the car's browser. Car mode only claims that one address: your other traffic and data are untouched, and other VPNs pause while it's on.

Drive safely. If you use DashCast with a car's screen, let a passenger operate it or use it while parked. Don't interact with it while driving.

Good to know

Apps that block capture

Apps that block screenshots, such as some banking and streaming apps, show black.

Navigation voice and calls

Android doesn't let apps capture navigation prompts or calls. In a car, connect the phone over Bluetooth for those, and leave DashCast's sound off to avoid hearing music twice.

Using DiPlay for CarPlay? In DiPlay's settings, set the audio stream to 3 (Android's media stream, the same number on every phone). Then its navigation prompts and music play as media sound, which DashCast can stream to the browser.

Same network

The phone and the browser must be on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot. DashCast doesn't work over the internet.
